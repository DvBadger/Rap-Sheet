RAP SHEET - BETA
================
Rap Sheet helps you mark suspected cheaters in a match, save a recording of it, and write up a report.

INSTALL
  1. Run RapSheet-Setup-<version>.exe. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because this beta is not code-signed
     yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Only do this if you got the file directly from the person who invited you.
  2. Follow the first-run wizard: pick your hotkey, your game window and the area where names appear.

USE
  Press your hotkey when you see something suspicious. Press it again to stop. Open the Report tab to finish the report.

PRIVACY
  Everything stays on your PC (Settings > Open data folder). Nothing is uploaded. Screen reading (OCR) is fully offline.

HEADS UP (read this)
  - Rap Sheet watches your hotkey globally and captures your screen/game window. Some anti-cheat systems may treat that
    as suspicious. It has been tested only in Rob Run with Easy Anti-Cheat for about 15 minutes. Test in casual play first
    and tell us what happens. You use the beta at your own risk.
  - Only tested on one PC so far. Expect rough edges.

SENDING FEEDBACK
  Settings > "Copy beta feedback", paste it into a message, and fill in the questions. It includes version, Windows,
  GPU and recent log lines (no screenshots or names are included).
  For games other than Rob Run / War Dogs, set Settings > Game name so reports are labeled correctly.

UNINSTALL
  Windows Settings > Apps > Rap Sheet > Uninstall. Your reports stay in the data folder unless you delete it.
